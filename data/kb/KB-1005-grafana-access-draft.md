# Requesting access to internal Grafana dashboards (DRAFT)

> Draft — pending review by the data platform team. Background reference only;
> steps below are not yet verified.

## Overview

Most employees already have `grafana:viewer` through their department group.
Editor/admin roles require a data platform approval.

## Draft steps

1. Check current entitlements — viewer access may already exist.
2. For editor access, the request goes to the data platform approvers queue with
   the manager in CC.
3. Provisioning is usually same-day after approval.
