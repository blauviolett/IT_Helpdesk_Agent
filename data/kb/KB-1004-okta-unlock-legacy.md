# Unlock Okta account via helpdesk portal (legacy)

> **DEPRECATED** — the legacy helpdesk portal was decommissioned in 2025.
> Use KB-1001 (self-service unlock verification) instead.

## Old procedure (do not use)

1. Open the legacy helpdesk portal at `helpdesk-legacy.corp.local`.
2. Search the employee by username and click "Force unlock".
3. Ask the employee to sign in within 5 minutes.
